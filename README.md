# 🇩🇪 Germany Election Analysis based on "Socio-Economic" factors

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes how socio-economic and demographic factors influence political voting patterns across Germany’s 16 federal states.

Using statistical techniques and visual analytics, the study identifies key drivers behind party vote share variations and regional political behavior.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To examine how factors such as income, unemployment, education, and demographics impact electoral outcomes and identify the most influential regions and voter groups.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- SAS Viya (Visual Analytics)
- Python (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- CSV datasets
- Statistical Modeling (Regression, Correlation Analysis)

---

## 📊 Dataset Sources
- Bundeswahlleiter (German Federal Election Data)
- Destatis (Economic Indicators)
- Eurostat (Demographic Data)
- Wikipedia (Supplementary data)

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. Data Collection from official sources  
2. Data Cleaning & Transformation (Python scripts)  
3. Feature Engineering (derived socio-economic indicators)  
4. Data Analysis using SAS Viya  
5. Visualization (dashboards, geo maps, scatter plots)  
6. Insights & Reporting  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Strong East–West Divide**  
  Eastern states show higher support for AfD, while Western regions lean toward Greens and CDU.

- **Economic Factors Matter**  
  Higher unemployment correlates with increased support for certain political parties.

- **Urban vs Rural Trends**  
  Urban, highly educated regions show strong support for Green Party.

- **Income & Stability Influence Voting**  
  Higher-income and economically stable regions tend to favor CDU/CSU.

- **Demographics Play a Key Role**  
  Age distribution, education levels, and foreign-born population significantly impact voting behavior.
